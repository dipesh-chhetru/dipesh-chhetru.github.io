About Me

My work combines forestry, field-based bird research and conservation to better understand threatened bird species and the ecological factors influencing their populations.

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Forestry at the Institute of Forestry, Pokhara Campus, Tribhuvan University, Nepal.

My interest in birds developed through field birdwatching and conservation activities and has increasingly developed into an interest in scientific research, particularly on raptors and vultures.

I am interested in developing strong skills in field research, ecological data analysis, GIS and scientific communication so that I can contribute to evidence- based bird conservation in Nepal.
