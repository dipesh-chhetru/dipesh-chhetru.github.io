Field Experience

Research Field Experiences

I have contributed as a field assistant and research assistant to several avian ecology and wildlife research projects in Nepal.

01

Avifaunal Habitat Association — Rupa Lake

Assisted Mr. Bipin Adhikari in field data collection for his Bachelor's thesis on avifaunal habitat association and anthropogenic impacts in Rupa Lake, Nepal.

02

Bird Diversity — Shankharnagar Community Forest

Worked as a Research Assistant to Mr. Bipin Adhikari on a project investigating bird diversity across different vegetation types of Shankharnagar Community Forest, Rupandehi, Nepal.

03

Avian Diversity — Great Machhapuchhre Trail

Assisted Ms. Shikshya Gayawali in field data collection for her Bachelor's thesis assessing avian diversity along the elevation gradient of the Great Machhapuchhre Trail, Kaski, Nepal, funded by Rising Explorers Grants.

04

Satyr Tragopan Research

Assisted Ms. Tulasi Darlami in field data collection and analysis for research on the distribution and conservation threats of Satyr Tragopan in Ghorepani, Nepal, funded by Rising Explorers Grants.

06

Avian Diversity in Catapult-Free Zones

Worked with Bird Conservation Nepal, Pokhara Branch, on the FON-supported project assessing avian diversity in catapult-free zones of Pokhara Metropolitan City and Myagdi District.

07

Egyptian Vulture Research

Assisted Mr. Ashish Pariyar in field data collection for his Bachelor's thesis on roost-tree characteristics, congregation and threats of Egyptian Vultures (Neophron percnopterus) in the Chitwan-Annapurna Landscape.

08

Importance of Large Old Trees for Avifauna

Assisted Mr. Mohan Bucha Magar with his research project investigating the importance of large old trees for avifauna in and around Pokhara Valley, funded by Friends of Nature (FON) Nepal.
