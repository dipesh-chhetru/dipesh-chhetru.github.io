My Background

From Forestry to Avian Ecology

I am a Bachelor of Science in Forestry student at the Institute of Forestry, Pokhara Campus, Tribhuvan University, Nepal. My academic background in forestry has developed my interest in ecosystems, biodiversity, wildlife and conservation.

My interest in birds grew through birdwatching and participation in conservation activities in and around Pokhara. Since then, I have increasingly focused on ornithology and avian ecology, particularly birds of prey and threatened species.

Through field assistant and research assistant roles, I have contributed to several studies involving avian diversity, habitat association, elevation gradients, raptor ecology and conservation threats across different landscapes of Nepal.
